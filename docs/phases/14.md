## 3. Facts measured

- The residue cache holds 500 sequences (`data/embeddings/0e528d4a46831b05_subset500_residues`), keyed by the corpus SHA-256 of the normalized sequence. 408 of them are real corpus sequences usable by the golden comparison; the gallery lists 11 (10 eukaryote, 1 plant).
- Golden inputs against the served engine: 67 refused (48 `not_in_embedding_cache`, 17 `unsupported_group`, 1 `invalid_sequence`, 1 `unknown_group`). The golden set therefore cannot test the served engine; it guards the motif module.
- Startup of the app with the real engine and index: 5.19 s.
- Parity (numpy against the torch training forward, 40 sequences over the length distribution): localization and GO sigmoids within 1e-5, CRF detection within 5e-4, cleavage site exact, signal posterior within 1e-4. The CRF detection difference measured over all 500 subset sequences is 1.2e-4 (§4 of ADR-0016).

## 5. Results

Time to result, split as ADR-0001 §4.3 asks (queue wait = `started_at - created_at`, compute = `finished_at - started_at`), through `/demo/predict` and `/demo/batch` with `MAX_BATCH_SEQUENCES` raised to 64 for the sweep only:

| Section | n | Queue wait p95 | Compute p95 | Time to result p95 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cold first request | 1 | 0.0 s | 2.844 s | 2.844 s |
| Sequential single jobs (3 rounds x 11 sequences) | 33 | 0.002 s | 2.587 s | 2.587 s (median 2.170 s) |
| Burst of 10 submitted together | 10 | 19.092 s | 2.596 s | 21.131 s (median 12.055 s) |

Batch sweep (3 repeats each, median compute):

| Sequences | Compute median | Per sequence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2.470 s | 2.470 s |
| 2 | 2.654 s | 1.327 s |
| 5 | 3.229 s | 0.646 s |
| 10 | 3.430 s | 0.343 s |
| 20 | 4.409 s | 0.220 s |
| 40 | 5.970 s | 0.149 s |

Read-off: a single job costs about 2.2 s, nearly all of it the MMseqs2 search loading the index (about 2 s per call, ADR-0016 §4.6); batching amortizes it. A 40-sequence batch is about 24,000 sequences per hour, sequential singles about 1,650 per hour (derived arithmetic, not measured). The burst shows the queue: ten simultaneous single jobs wait up to 19 s, which is what `estimated_wait_seconds` is for. All sections: 0 failed, 0 degraded evidence.

`MAX_BATCH_SEQUENCES=20`: half the largest batch measured (40), because the sweep reuses 10 distinct eukaryote sequences cyclically and the real host has fewer cores. 20 sequences take a median 4.4 s on this machine.

ADR-0001 §4.3 targets: every measured p95 is within them on this machine; this says nothing about the host.

Served against the motif engine on the 408 cached real sequences (397 comparable; 11 are outside the motif engine's 30-3000 range and were counted, not crashed on): the top location is the same for 54.2% (`data/serving/served_vs_motif.json`, gitignored). This is an agreement rate between two methods, not an accuracy.

3. Consistency rule R06 is inactive (Phase 13 hits carry no location); `rules_version` is still "demo-0.1.0".
4. The gallery is in-sample.

8. The `/demo/reference` endpoint was removed with the synthetic reference data it served. `vite.config.ts` gained a `/demo` proxy.
9. `Dockerfile.serve` was not updated; the `serve` extra exists in `backend/pyproject.toml`. `.env.example` was not edited.
10. mypy `ignore_errors` overrides for the legacy modules `inference/motif.py`, `features/classical.py` and `data/reference_demo.py` (37 errors that predate this phase). New modules are strict.
11. `GOA_RELEASE` is dropped from the serving contract (never read).

## 10. Noticed, not changed

- The search loads the index on every call (about 2 s); a resident search server would remove most of the latency. Not built.
- `streamlit` pins an older starlette than the one installed (a pip warning only).
- Windows PowerShell 5.1 `Set-Content -Encoding utf8` writes a byte-order mark; `.env` had one and hid `MODEL_REGISTRY`. `serving.py` now reads with `utf-8-sig`.
- The file sync between the working container and the repository lagged by 10-30 s and sometimes needed a second write; every file was re-checked by SHA-256 on the device before being relied on.
- The long-sequence (over 1022) path of the encoder is untouched, since the encoder is cache-only.
