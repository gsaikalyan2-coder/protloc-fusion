### Corpus beside the plan's scale

| Quantity | Plan target | Corpus | Corpus / target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sequences: all records | 25,000 | 548,702 | 21.9x |
| Sequences: localization annotated (all) | 25,000 | 152,124 | 6.1x |
| Sequences: localization annotated (ECO:0000269) | 25,000 | 36,730 | 1.5x |
| Localization classes | 10 | 10 |  |
| Membrane-type classes | 4 | 8 |  |
| Signal classes (one per signal task here) | 9 | 3 |  |

Differences from the plan's scale (targets, not numbers to hit; no filter was tuned):

- **Sequences.** The corpus is 548,702 records, 21.9x the plan's ~25k. The closest like-for-like figure is
  eukaryotic localization-annotated: 152,124 (all) and 36,730 (ECO:0000269 only), 6.1x and 1.5x.
  Likely reasons, **UNVERIFIED** against DeepLoc's own method: this corpus applies no evidence filter (all codes kept, ADR-0006 §3.5),
  no redundancy reduction (Phase 6), no fragment or length exclusion, and covers all four groups.
- **Localization classes:** 10 vs 10.
- **Membrane classes:** 8 vs the plan's 4. The label map splits single-pass by type I to IV, and keeps lipid anchor, peripheral and multi-pass.
  Collapsing is a modelling choice for a later phase; the corpus keeps the finer classes.
- **Signal classes:** 3 per-type labels (signal peptide, mitochondrial and chloroplast transit peptide) vs the plan's 9.
  The other signal types are not in `uniprot_label_map.yaml`; adding them would change the corpus hash by design.
