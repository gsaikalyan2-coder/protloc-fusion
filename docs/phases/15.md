# Phase 15: Attribution from the real model

Branch `phase/15-attribution`, from `main` (the Phase 14 merge, 096630b). Spec: `PROJECT_PLAN.md` §4 row 15. Decisions: `docs/adr/ADR-0017-model-attribution.md`.

## 3. Facts measured

Attention in each head is shared across classes (computed before the final layer), so raw attention cannot be class-specific. Position bias is reported per relative-position decile (ratio to uniform) in `attribution_facts.json`.

## 4. Parity, refusal and independence checks

Occlusion equals a float64 hand recompute; attention is class-agnostic; plan_windows covers all residues within the cap and refuses a cap below 2; CRF track zero-pads beyond the cap; `engine.json` settings equal the frozen protocol; `AttributionMethod` values equal `METHODS` and each has a claim; the module imports only numpy and the two head modules.

## 5. Results

Mean deletion drop at k = 5/10/20/40 %:

| Head | Method | k=5 | k=10 | k=20 | k=40 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| localization | occlusion | 0.058 | 0.085 | 0.112 | 0.136 |
| localization | pooling attention | 0.045 | 0.077 | 0.114 | 0.149 |
| localization | motif | 0.022 | 0.047 | 0.074 | 0.098 |
| GO | occlusion | 0.130 | 0.184 | 0.227 | 0.253 |
| GO | pooling attention | 0.165 | 0.217 | 0.261 | 0.286 |

Random removal about 0 to 0.013. Bottom-k occlusion is negative (localization -0.020 to -0.048; GO -0.036 to -0.087). Every lower bound of (drop minus random) is positive. Signal peptide AUROC: CRF 0.9990 [0.9965, 0.9999], motif hydropathy 0.863 [0.805, 0.889], position-only prior 0.9936 [0.9896, 0.9964].

Serving re-measure vs Phase 14: single p95 3.523 s (was 2.587), burst time-to-result p95 21.955 s (was 21.131; queue wait 19.72 s, compute 2.752 s), cold first request 2.732 s, startup 5.39 s. Batch medians for 1/2/5/10/20/40: 2.492/2.509/3.447/4.205/5.837/11.403 s; per sequence 0.292 s at 20 and 0.285 s at 40 (was 0.149). Attribution adds about 0.14 s per sequence in batches. `MAX_BATCH_SEQUENCES` stays 20 (half of 40).

## 8. Deviations

1. GO attribution is validated, not served (no UI for it).
2. Hydropathy, membrane type and the scanner remain `source: "motif"`.
3. At k = 20 and 40 pooling attention drops the probability more than occlusion; the default stays occlusion because it is class-specific and signed.
4. `MAX_BATCH_SEQUENCES` unchanged at 20 although per-sequence cost roughly doubled.

## 9. UNVERIFIED

- Any claim beyond the 500 cached sequences or two served groups; gallery sequences are in-sample.
- Biological meaning of any track; the deletion test is faithfulness to the head only.
- Whether the single-job p95 shift (2.587 to 3.523 s, n = 33) is real or noise.
- Host timing, Docker, GitHub Actions.
- How much of the CRF's signal is position (the position-only prior reaches 0.9936).
- The frontend rendering was built and linted, not reviewed by eye in this write-up.
