## 3. Facts measured

- Raw decisions reproduce Phase 9 exactly: pooled validation macro F1 0.4763 [0.4279, 0.5162], Jaccard 0.4696 (`--evaluate` fails otherwise).
- Peroxisome has 6 positives. Its method is Platt in the frozen fit and in two folds, `uncalibrated` in folds 1 and 3 (positives drop under 5); the manifest marks it `consistent: false`.
- Calibration is monotone per label, so ranking (AUC-type metrics) is unchanged; only probabilities and thresholds move.
- Calibrated thresholds, class order of `baseline_class_map.yaml`: 0.667, 0.368, 0.182, 0.500, 0.085, 0.157, 0.333, 0.482, 0.042, 0.119.
- Phase 9 raw thresholds are superseded for serving (ADR-0012).

## 4. Leakage and protocol checks

- A unit test flips the scored fold's labels and asserts calibrators and thresholds for that fold are unchanged.
- The frozen thresholds equal `tune_thresholds` on the cross-fitted calibrated scores, not on in-sample scores (tested).
- Partition 5 is never loaded; there is no `--test` mode.
- `calibrate.py --check` passes without data bytes.
- The bundle loader refuses other head weights, a tampered calibrator, a changed rule file, an edited bundle document and a missing file (tested).

## 8. Deviations

1. **Cross-fitting on Phase 9's out-of-fold scores** instead of a separate calibration split (your gate B choice): the subset is too small to spare one.
2. **Doubly nested thresholds** with the inner calibrator forced to the outer method, so fold thresholds never see the scored fold.
3. **Registry bundle plus loader** (`protloc.registry.bundle`, your gate C choice); `calibration` and `registry` cannot import `data` (layering), so `canonical_json_bytes` is duplicated and a test asserts equality.
4. **No new dependency** (gate A): isotonic (pool-adjacent-violators) and Platt (Newton with step halving) are written in the repo.
5. **Eligibility 20 / 5 / else uncalibrated** and **10 equal-mass bins**, from `configs/training/calibration.yaml` (gate B3).
6. **`--smoke` runs** (every 4th row) tested the code; no number from them appears here.
7. **A stale `.git/index.lock`** (0 bytes) was deleted after your approval (gate E).
